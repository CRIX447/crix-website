CloudScript needs deploying

Granting roles, banning and friend requests all run on PlayFab's servers. Until the script is deployed there, those buttons will keep failing.

  1. Press Copy CloudScript below
  2. Open PlayFab → your title → Automation → CloudScript → Revisions
  3. Select everything in the editor and paste over it
  4. Click Save as revision
  5. Click Deploy to live — this step is the one people miss; saving alone does nothing
  6. Come back and press Re-check

The live site is out of date

  1. Open Vercel → your project → Deployments. Is the newest one from your last push?
  2. Settings → Git — if it can't see the repository, click Reconnect.
  3. On GitHub: Settings → Applications → Vercel → Configure, grant access to the repo.
  4. Back on Vercel, press Redeploy on the latest commit.
