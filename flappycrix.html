❓ HOW TO PLAY
🐦

Keep flying

Tap the screen, press SPACE, or press A on a controller to flap. Every tap gives you a little lift — gravity does the rest.

🪙

Collect coins

Coins float between the pipes. Spend them in the Store on hats, shirts and power-ups. Some cosmetics only unlock at higher levels.

🌐

Play with others

Hit MULTIPLAYER to join a lobby. Everyone flies the same course, and arrows on the edge of the screen show who is ahead or behind you.

🏆

Climb the ranks

Casual is for fun. Competitive awards rank points based on where you finish — Bronze up to Unreal. Ranked needs at least two players.

👥

Add friends

Share your friend code from the Friends tab. Once they accept you can invite each other to lobbies and jump straight into their game.
