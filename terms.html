Flappy Crix is free to play. You may use it for personal enjoyment. By playing you agree to these terms — if you do not, please stop using it.

You are responsible for what happens on your account. Do not share your sign-in details. Tell us if you think someone else has access.

3. Behaviour

Do not do any of the following:

Coins and cosmetics have no real-world value and cannot be exchanged for money. They are not currently sold for real money. If your account is banned you lose access to them.

Use the in-game report button, or email cgvr@crixgamingvr.com. Reports are reviewed by hand.

6. Appeals

If you think a ban was a mistake, email us with your player ID. We will look at it properly, though the decision after review is final.

8. Age

You must be at least 13 to make an account. If you are under 16, please check with a parent or guardian before signing up.
